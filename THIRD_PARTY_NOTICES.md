# Credits and redistribution notices

OMG Editor is a derivative of the [Three.js editor](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r183/editor). Its scene model, property panels, command architecture, loaders, exporters, and much of its UI come from that project. NukeItGames maintains the OMG-specific editing and desktop changes. This repository does not claim independent authorship of the underlying editor or rendering engine.
